Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tennis Lab ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok accounts (the "Service").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Tennis Lab does not operate its own website, app, or sign-up form, and we do not directly collect, store, or process personal data such as names, email addresses, or payment information from our followers.

2. Information Handled by Third-Party Platforms

When you follow, message, or interact with our accounts on Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok, those platforms collect and process your data according to their own privacy policies, not ours:

  • Telegram Privacy Policy

We only see aggregate, platform-provided analytics (e.g. view counts, follower counts) made available to account administrators by those platforms; we do not receive or store individually identifying data about you from them beyond what you choose to share with us directly (for example, in a comment or direct message).

3. Direct Messages & Comments

If you message or comment to us directly, we may read and respond to that message. We do not sell or share the content of your messages with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and due to its gambling-related content is intended for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights over any personal data held about you by the third-party platforms listed above — please refer to their own privacy tools and policies to exercise those rights (e.g. via your Telegram, Instagram/Facebook, or TikTok account settings).

6. Changes to This Policy
